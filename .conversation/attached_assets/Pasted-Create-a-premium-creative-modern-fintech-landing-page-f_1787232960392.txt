Create a premium, creative, modern fintech landing page for **RePay for Business**, a Saudi payment technology company.

The website should feel like a combination of:

* Modern fintech
* Premium SaaS
* Open Banking
* Financial infrastructure
* Minimal but highly creative product design

Avoid the typical generic fintech landing page look. The design should feel **innovative, confident, clean, interactive, and premium**, with strong visual hierarchy and subtle motion.

## BRAND

Brand: **RePay**

Primary color:
`#008CFF`

Secondary / dark color:
`#2D323A`

Arabic font:
**Rubik**

English font:
**Nunito**

Use Arabic as the primary language for the page, but make the design fully bilingual-ready.

Use generous whitespace, large typography, rounded cards, subtle gradients, glass-like surfaces where appropriate, and modern fintech UI elements.

Do NOT overuse gradients, shadows, or decorative elements.

# FAQ SECTION

Create a clean, modern FAQ section using expandable accordion cards.

Section title:

**الأسئلة الشائعة**

Include these questions:

### 01

**ما هي RePay؟**

Answer should explain that RePay provides businesses with a new payment method that allows customers to pay directly from their bank accounts using Open Banking technology.

### 02

**كيف يتم الدفع من الحساب البنكي مباشرة؟**

Explain the flow simply:
Customer selects RePay → reviews payment details → authenticates through their bank → payment is confirmed.

### 03

**هل بياناتي آمنة؟**

Explain that RePay uses Open Banking technology and secure authentication through the customer's bank, without requiring the customer to share their banking login credentials with RePay.

### 04

**هل توجد طرق دفع أخرى غير الحساب البنكي؟**

Explain that RePay can work alongside existing payment methods and does not require merchants to replace their current payment options.

### 05

**هل RePay يستبدل بوابة الدفع الحالية؟**

Explain that RePay is designed as an additional payment method that can complement existing payment methods and payment infrastructure.

Use:

`#008CFF`

Dark:
`#2D323A`

Background:
Mostly white / very light neutral backgrounds.

Typography:
Arabic → **Rubik**
English → **Nunito**

Typography hierarchy should be bold and editorial.

Hero headline should be very large.

Use:

* Large headlines
* Short paragraphs
* Rounded cards
* 16–28px corner radius
* Soft shadows
* Subtle borders
* Minimal gradients
* Large whitespace

Avoid:

* Generic corporate stock photography
* Generic blue illustrations
* Excessive icons
* Excessive rounded pills
* Traditional navbar
* Busy layouts
* Too many colors
* Cheap-looking gradients

# RESPONSIVE DESIGN

The website must be fully responsive.

Desktop:
Large cinematic layouts with strong visual hierarchy.

Tablet:
Maintain spacious layouts while reducing visual complexity.

Mobile:

* Centered logo
* Minimal language selector
* Large readable hero
* Stacked CTAs
* Vertical payment flow
* Feature cards stacked vertically
* BNPM section optimized for mobile
* FAQ accordion full width

Ensure Arabic RTL is implemented correctly.
